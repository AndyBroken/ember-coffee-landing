Our Story

A little corner shop with a big love for coffee.

Ember started as a tiny espresso cart at the Saturday farmers market. Ten years later we're still the same family-run crew — just with more seats, a roaster in the back room and a lot more regulars.

We work directly with farms in Colombia, Ethiopia and Guatemala, roast in small batches and brew every cup like it's for a friend. Because around here, it usually is.

— Maria & Josh, owners
